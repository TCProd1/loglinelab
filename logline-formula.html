The logline formula

When [inciting incident], a [specific, flawed protagonist] must [goal] before [stakes / ticking clock].

Variations:

  • [Protagonist] must [goal] or else [consequence].
  • After [event], [protagonist] [action] - but [ironic complication].

Keep it 25-45 words, present tense, no names, and run it through the LoglineLab checker.
